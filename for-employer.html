1. Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO)

When it comes time to explore the Hiring process considering EMTA's Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO) services you can make the process more effective, and easily linked to traditional and non-traditional hiring support, as well full recruitment service support, which supports organizations in attracting, engaging, and retaining the right people. We can take over every detail and step in the process from sourcing talent, screening, skills testing, and onboarding all using a data-supported solution with a streamlined approach, so you can focus on growing your organization rather than managing your people.

EMTA recognizes that retention doesn't happen without full-time job effort and management. Improving retention is very difficult, and costly. That is one of the reason we designed the RPO services to decrease the time to fill, decrease the cost per hire, and improve the quality of hire. Through the combination of technology, proven methodology, hundreds of strong wrapped industry relationships, we engage, attract, and identify top talent that aligns with your unique cultural style and experience. We support organizations in hiring or onboarding professionals at any scale, whether that means hiring and onboarding a few, or an individual or a massive organization or individual onboarding project, all utilizing recruiters that are also professionals.

3. Third Party Payroll Management

At the Elite Manpower and Training Academy (EMTA), we take pride in the fully customized, comprehensive Third-Party Payroll Management services we are able to provide to employers in all industries. We understand that the complexity of different organizational structures mandates a thorough understanding of applicable statutory regulations and compliance as they relate to Third- Party Payroll Management. Our expert team will work hand-in-hand with your business to develop customized payroll solutions that are accurate, efficient and compliant.

EMTA's end-to-end payroll services includes every aspect of salary management: Salary Calculation, Processing, Tax Deductions, Statutory Contributions, Employee Benefits, and other important related payroll services. We work with you to ensure that all payroll cycles are error-free and timely prepared according to the latest statutory guidelines, allowing employers the peace of mind associated with the security of compliant payroll solutions and the process efficiencies that come with the use of Third-Party Payroll Management solutions. By working with EMTA, you will be freeing up time that was otherwise spent on payroll processes and can now focus on moving your organization to the next level by focusing on growth, not on day-to-day payroll process responsibilities. While EMTA manages your payroll, you can be rest assured that the full payroll management process will run smoothly and transparently each pay cycle, maintaining compliance while ensuring employee satisfaction. EMTA - your trusted and reliable partner in Payroll, assisting with the incredible journey to build an organized, efficient and industry-ready workforce instead of worrying.
