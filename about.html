BFSI Sector

The BFSI (Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance) sector is one of the most dynamic and rapidly expanding industries globally. Whether you're just starting or you're an experienced professional, the sector offers numerous career opportunities across various roles—from banking operations to insurance management. With EMTA by your side, you can find the right jobs at BFSI that match your skills and aspirations. Explore various positions, enhance your career, and become part of a thriving industry with EMTA's support. The BFSI sector (Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance) offers a wide range of jobs for individuals looking to build a career in finance and related industries. Jobs in BFSI span across various roles, including banking operations, investment analysis, insurance underwriting, risk management, and financial advisory. With the increasing demand for financial services, there are numerous career opportunities available, from entry-level positions to managerial roles. The BFSI industry provides stability, growth potential, and competitive salaries, making it an attractive choice for job seekers.

Founder's Story & Journey

Abhijeet Raghuwanshi

Elite Manpower and Training Academy (EMTA) was founded in 2020 by Abhijeet Raghuwanshi with a clear and singular vision: to bridge the gap between ambitious young job seekers and the rapidly growing BFSI (Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance) sector in India.

Having witnessed the struggles of fresh graduates in Indore who possessed potential but lacked the practical, industry-aligned training required by banks and corporate employers, Abhijeet set out to create a training ecosystem that is not just educational, but career-transforming.

Why EMTA was Started

EMTA was established as a beacon of hope and guidance for freshers. The traditional academic curriculum often leaves a gap when it comes to hands-on customer acquisition, credit analysis, loan documentation, and corporate etiquette. EMTA addresses this by delivering intensive, role-specific programs taught by veteran industry experts, backed by a 100% placement assurance.

Our Vision for the Future
