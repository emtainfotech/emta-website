What is Banking (BFSI)?

Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance (BFSI) is a comprehensive term used to refer to the industry encompassing bank's institutions, financial services firms, and insurance companies. These sectors are integral parts of the global economy, providing a wide range of services to individuals, businesses, and governments.

BFSI Training

Banking involves financial institutions that accept deposits from the public and provide various financial services, including lending, investment, and payment services. bank's play a crucial role in facilitating economic activities by channeling funds from savers to borrowers and providing liquidity in the financial system.

Financial Services

Financial services encompass a broad range of activities provided by specialized firms, including investment banking, asset management, brokerage services, wealth management, and financial advisory services. These firms help individuals and organizations manage their finances, invest in financial markets, and navigate complex financial transactions.

Insurance

Insurance companies offer risk management solutions by providing financial protection against various risks, such as accidents, illnesses, property damage, and liability. Individuals and businesses purchase insurance policies to transfer the financial consequences of potential losses to the insurance provider in exchange for premium payments.

Program Terms & Conditions

Refund Policy

If EMTA is unable to place a candidate within the committed period, a refund of paid fees (except enrolment charges) will be initiated.

Non-Residential Program

This program is designed as a non-residential BFSI training initiative. Candidates are responsible for their own food and lodging arrangements for offline courses.

Unlimited Interview Opportunities

Avail unlimited interview opportunities during the refund period, maximizing your chances of securing a suitable position.

Certification

Upon successful completion of the course, candidates receive a certification for their respective courses.

Job Location Flexibility

While we cannot guarantee specific job locations post-placement, our efforts are directed toward securing positions in your preferred locations.

Selection Process

Step 1: Application Registration

Initiate the process by completing the online/offline application form either on this platform or at our office.

Step 2: Two-Stage Proctored Online Assessments

Level 1: Online Test on Google Forms

  • Subjects: Banking Awareness, General Studies, Economics of India, Analytical Ability, Numerical Ability, and English Test.

Level 2: Video Interview/In-person Interview

  • Online Assessment conducted via video calling platforms or offline one-to-one interview.
  • Successful candidates qualify for a complimentary 1-day demonstration of the finalized course.

Step 3: Admission and Enrolment

Upon selection, candidates receive an admission letter upon their agreement to join the EMTA BFSI training program. After completing admission formalities, selected candidates are officially enrolled in the training course.

Conditions for Candidature Cancellation

  • Failure to meet eligibility criteria, submission of incorrect/false information, or suppression of material facts at any stage, even after admission/appointment, will result in the cancellation of candidature.
  • If deemed ineligible for rehiring by a Financial Institution (FI), the application will be canceled.
  • The FI’s decision on the candidate’s eligibility is final, and no correspondence will be entertained.
