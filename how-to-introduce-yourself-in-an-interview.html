How to Introduce Yourself in an Interview

EMTA Admin 27 Jun 2026

Walking into a job interview can be really exciting and very stressful. You have probably prepared for the questions researched the company and updated your resume.. When the interviewer smiles and says, "Tell me about yourself " many people suddenly become nervous.

The truth is that your introduction is one of the parts of any interview. It makes people think of you in a way and sets the tone for the rest of the conversation.

If you are new to this or getting ready, for a job interview this guide will help you learn how to introduce yourself in a way what to say and what not to say.

This guide will also give you examples of introductions that you can use for your own job interviews.

When you are at a job interview introducing yourself is really important. The people who are talking to you will usually say something like tell me a bit about yourself. They want to know more about the kind of person you're what you are like not just what is written on your resume, about your job experience and education. They want to learn more about you.

They want to see how well you can talk about yourself and if you can do it in a way. They are looking at how you talk about the school you went to what you are good at and the skills you have.

They also want to know what you want to do with your career. If you are a good fit, for the job you are applying for. Introducing yourself is a part of the interview and it is something that the interviewers pay a lot of attention to because introducing yourself is one thing that can make you stand out from other people who are applying for the same job and introducing yourself is what shows the interviewers who you are and what you can do.

A good introduction does not guarantee a job. It can create a positive first impression that helps throughout the interview. So what should you include in your introduction? A simple and effective introduction usually includes five points:

1. Your name. Start with your name.
Example: Good morning sir. My name is Rahul Sharma.

2. Educational background. I. Got a degree from a university.
For example I completed my Bachelor of Commerce degree from Devi Ahilya University in Indore.

3.. Strengths. Here are some skills I have that match the job.
For example my skills include being able to talk to people knowing things, about computers and being able to learn new things quickly like communication skills.

4. Experience or projects. Freshers can mention projects, internships, certifications or practical training.
Example: During my year I worked on a marketing project that helped me understand customer behavior and teamwork.

5. Career goals. When you finish talking make sure to say what you want.
For instance you can say that you want a job where you can learn and grow as a professional and help the company do well.

You can follow a plan: talk about your name, what you studied what you are good at what you have done before and what you want to do in your career.

This will help you stay on track and feel good about yourself when you are talking to someone, about a job.

Here are some examples of how you can start talking about yourself when you want a job. For freshers:

Good morning. My name is priyanshu jat. I just finished my Bcom degree from XYZ University.

In college I really liked doing presentations and group discussions and projects. I did a lot of presentations and group discussions and projects. These presentations and group discussions and projects helped me get better at talking to people and working with people. I got to talk to a lot of people and work with a lot of people, on these presentations and group discussions and projects.

I think I learn fast. I like trying new things. I know how to use Microsoft Office and do business communication.

I'm new, to the job market. I'm looking for a chance to use what I know learn more and have a great career.

Thank you.

For BPO jobs:

Good morning sir/madam.My name is Neha Sharma. I finished my graduation in Commerce. I can talk well. I love meeting new people.

During my studies I did activities that helped me become more confident and good at solving problems.

I am okay with working in a team. I can handle customer conversations in a professional way.

I am really looking forward to starting my career, in the BPO industry.

I think my communication skills and positive attitude will help me do a job and add value to the organization.

Thank you.

For customer care executive:

Good morning sir/madam. My name is Rakesh Patel.I finished my Bachelors degree in 2025.

I like talking to people and helping them fix their issues.

I am good at listening I am patient. I always think about the customer.

In college I did team projects which helped me work well with others.

I am excited, about a customer service job where I can learn get experience and make customers happy.

Thank you.

For jobs:

Good morning sir/madam. My name is Pooja Verma. I finished my studies at ABC College. I feel I am good at talking to people. I like speaking with people. I am okay with using computers. I can pick up computer systems fast. I think my positive attitude, patience and willingness to learn will help me do well in a telecalling role at a company. I really want to start working and learn more about the company. I want to do a job, in the telecalling role. I am excited to begin my journey and grow with the organization. Thank you.

For IT jobs:

Good morning sir/madam.My name is Saurabh Singh. I just finished my Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science. When I was studying I did projects that had to do with making websites and taking care of databases. I know about HTML and CSS and JavaScript and basic programming concepts. I really like finding out about technologies and figuring out technical problems, with Computer Science. I think Computer Science is very interesting and I like solving problems related to Computer Science.. As a fresher I am looking for an opportunity to apply my skills gain industry experience and grow as an IT professional. Thank you.

To deliver an introduction remember to maintain eye contact speak clearly keep it short smile naturally and practice before the interview. Avoid speaking fast memorizing every word giving irrelevant family details using overly complex language mentioning weaknesses in your introduction and talking for more than two minutes.

If you don't have work experience don't worry. IInterviewers know that freshers are new to their careers.

They look at what you have achieved your college projects, any certifications you have how well you communicate, any team work experience you have and how eager you're to learn.

Employers think attitude and ability to learn are just as important as experience.

When you are getting ready, for an interview make sure you learn about the company.

Read the job details carefully.

Go over your resume.

Prepare answers to questions that HR usually asks.

Wear clothes and get to the interview on time.

The more you prepare the more confident you will feel.

People often ask about self-introduction in an interview. One common question is how long a self-introduction should be.

1. The answer to this question is that your self-introduction in an interview should be pretty short. Ideally your self-introduction in an interview should last, between 60 and 90 seconds.

2. Should freshers mention family details? No family details are usually not necessary unless specifically asked by the interviewer.

3. Can I memorize my introduction? You should understand the structure and key points. Avoid memorizing every word. Speak naturally.

4.. When you want to introduce yourself start with a hello.

Say your name clearly.

Then talk about where you studied.

This is a way to begin.

You can say something, like "Hi I'm Your Name] I studied at [Your School/College]."

It is easy and friendly.

5. What can I do when I feel nervous?

Take a breath smile and speak slowly.

The people interviewing me know that I might feel nervous.

6. Can I talk about my hobbies when I introduce myself. Yes,. Only if they are relevant and there is enough time. Focus on education, skills and career goals.

In the end your introduction is a chance to show people what you are like and how well you can communicate and be confident.

A good introduction helps the people interviewing you understand who you are and why you are a person for the job.

You should keep your introduction simple and professional. Talk about the things you are good at.

Practice a lot. Try to be confident.

Remember that every interview is a chance to learn something

If you are trying to get a job in a call center or as a customer care person or a telecaller or a job in the IT field a good introduction can help you make an impression and get closer to the job you want.
